6. DATA STORAGE AND PRIVACY

The Application is designed on a local-first basis.

  a) All data you enter is stored on your device, in a database and photo files held
     in the Application's own storage. It is not transmitted anywhere except through
     the optional features described in this Section and in Sections 7 and 8, each of
     which is off until you switch it on. The update checks described in Section 10
     send no part of it.

  b) The Application does not require an account and does not collect or store any
     personal information as part of its normal operation. The Developer operates no
     server holding your records and has no copy of your collection.

  c) You are solely responsible for maintaining backups of your data. The Application
     provides export functions for this purpose, including a full database snapshot on
     desktop. The Developer accepts no liability for data loss resulting from device
     failure, uninstallation, or any other cause.

  d) If you choose to export a job to your device calendar (see Section 9), the text of
     that job and the tree or collection it belongs to are written into a calendar
     entry, which leaves the Application's own storage and is thereafter held by your
     device's calendar. Where that calendar belongs to an account you have configured to
     synchronise — an iCloud or similar account — your operating system may replicate
     the entry to that account's servers, under that provider's terms rather than these.
     The Developer neither performs nor receives any such transfer. On Android the
     Application's calendar is created as a local, non-synchronising one. Export is
     off unless you turn it on, one job at a time.

  e) Frost warnings are off unless you turn them on in Settings. While they are on,
     the Application sends the name you type when choosing a town, with the
     Application's language, to GeoNames (geonames.org) to find it, and that town's
     position, rounded to about a kilometre, to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute
     (api.met.no) for its weather forecast. No tree, species, or identifier of you is
     sent, and the chosen town stays on the device you chose it on. Your IP address
     reaches each service as it would when contacting any server. Both services'
     data is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence and credited in
     the Application. A frost warning is general guidance derived from a forecast,
     not a guarantee of the conditions your trees will meet.


7. CLOUD BACKUP AND SYNC (OPTIONAL)

The Application can back up your collection to, and synchronise it between your own
devices through, a Dropbox or Microsoft OneDrive account that you supply (the
"storage provider"). This feature is disabled until you connect an account from the
Backup & Sync screen.

  a) If you connect an account, a file containing your records and copies of your
     photographs and videos are uploaded to a folder in your own storage provider
     account.

  b) The data is held in your storage provider account, not by the Developer. The
     Developer has no access to it and does not receive a copy.

  c) The connection is authorised by you through the storage provider's own sign-in
     flow. The resulting access tokens are held in your device's secure credential
     store and are never transmitted to the Developer.

  d) You may disconnect at any time. The Application also provides an action to delete
     the backup held in your storage provider account. Disconnecting does not delete
     data already stored there; deleting the backup does not affect the copy on your
     device.

  e) The storage provider's own terms and privacy policy govern its handling of
     anything stored in your account. They are available at
     https://www.dropbox.com/privacy for Dropbox and
     https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement for Microsoft OneDrive.


8. CRASH REPORTING (OPTIONAL)

The mobile Application includes an optional crash and performance reporting feature
powered by Sentry (sentry.io). It is disabled by default and requires your explicit
consent before it is activated (Settings > App > Anonymous crash reports). It is
not present in the desktop version.

  a) If you enable it, diagnostic data — including crash stack traces, device model,
     operating system version, and app version — may be transmitted to Sentry's
     servers.

  b) Nothing is recorded or reported unless something goes wrong. Sessions in which no
     error occurs are not recorded. A small proportion of sessions additionally report
     timing measurements, which contain no recording and no content.

  c) When an error does occur, a replay of the interface in the moments leading up to
     it is included. This is a reconstruction of the screen layout, the navigation
     between screens, and where you tapped — not a video of the display. Text and
     images are masked, so the words in your diary entries and the content of your
     photographs do not appear in it.

  d) Your diary entries, tree records, photographs, and videos are not transmitted as
     part of a crash report.

  e) Your IP address reaches Sentry's servers in the course of sending a report, as it
     would when contacting any server. The Application does not attach it to the report,
     and Sentry is configured not to store it.

  f) You may withdraw consent at any time by disabling the toggle in Settings >
     App. The change takes effect the next time you open the Application.

  g) Sentry's own privacy policy governs Sentry's processing of any data transmitted
     to its platform and is available at https://sentry.io/privacy/.


9. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

The Application may request the following device permissions, each of which is used
solely for the features described:

  Camera             — photographing and recording video of your collection, trees,
                       pots, and species, and scanning printed QR labels.
  Photo Library      — adding existing photographs and videos to your records.
  Microphone         — recording the audio track of videos, and voice input for
                       diary and job entries.
  Speech Recognition — transcribing voice input into text.
  Notifications      — reminders for scheduled jobs and backup prompts. These are
                       scheduled on your device; the Application has no push
                       notification server and sends you nothing remotely.
  Exact Alarms       — on Android, delivering a job reminder at the time you set
                       rather than at a time chosen by the system.
  Calendar           — adding jobs you choose to export to your device calendar.
                       Granted only if you use that feature.

The Application creates and uses its own calendar, named "Inchgrove", and confines
itself to that calendar: it adds, updates, and removes only the entries it created
there for jobs you chose to export, and it does not read, list, or alter events in any
other calendar on your device. Nothing from your calendar is transmitted anywhere.

Your operating system may present this as a single, broad calendar permission covering
both reading and writing. That is the only tier the platforms offer an application that
maintains a calendar of its own — a narrower write-only permission exists on recent iOS
but cannot create or address a calendar, and Android offers no such tier at all. The
limitation described in the preceding paragraph is a commitment by the Developer, not
one enforced by your device. You may revoke the permission at any time; job export then
stops working and nothing else is affected.

Speech recognition is carried out by your device's operating system or, in the desktop
version, by the embedded browser engine. Depending on your platform, version, and
settings, the audio may be transcribed on the device or sent to Apple's or Google's
speech services for transcription. In either case it is handled by the platform and
not by the Developer, who neither receives nor stores it. If you would rather no audio
left your device, do not use the voice input feature.

Permissions may be refused or revoked at any time in your device settings; the feature
that depends on the permission then stops working, and the rest of the Application is
unaffected.


10. SOFTWARE UPDATES

The mobile Application, in either Edition, may check for and download updates
automatically through Expo's update service, and apply them the next time it starts.
This request discloses your IP address and your app and device version to Expo. No part
of Your Content is involved.

Such updates cover part of the Application only. A change requiring a new installation
package is not delivered this way: for a Store Edition it arrives through the platform
store, and for a directly installed Android package you obtain and install the newer
package yourself.

The desktop Application does not update itself. Updates are obtained by downloading and
installing a newer version.

The desktop Application and the directly installed Android package can tell you when a
newer version is released. Unless you turn this off in Settings, they fetch a small
file listing the latest versions from GitHub (github.com) about once a day. The request
carries no identifier and none of Your Content, though your IP address reaches GitHub as
it would when contacting any server. Nothing is downloaded or installed by this check.
